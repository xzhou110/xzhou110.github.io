Apartment Shopping

Open App ↗View Source ↗

A Key DecisionKeep personal notes intact when the listing catalog changes.

The Case Study

Problem
Hunting a short-term Bay Area lease across Zillow, Craigslist, Apartments.com and building sites, browser tabs and spreadsheets lost context: no photos, no distance to what mattered, no record of what had been contacted, toured or ruled out.
What I Built
A rental shortlist with lease fit, availability, market comparisons, risk flags, search status, and side-by-side comparison. Offline location lookup makes distance comparisons possible without a geocoding request for every listing.
How It Works
The pure engine handles ranking, geocoding, filters, and export with an injectable clock. A seed catalog and browser-stored overlay use stable identifiers so catalog updates do not erase notes.
Where It Stands
Built for my own rental search and publicly available as a single-user app. It reuses a proven comparison workflow while adapting the data model to a different decision.
Evidence and Limits
  • Distance is straight-line, not driving or transit time. Market comparisons are estimates.
  • Listings are curated with assistant support. Scam and income-restriction flags are recorded at capture time, not detected by an in-app AI service.
  • The test report and review findings provide dated evidence; engine tests do not establish every UI behavior.
