Private Desktop App

A private reader that organizes ideas from long-form videos into topic pages, with quotations linked to their source timestamps.

Local Knowledge App · Private Source

Key DecisionCompute quotation locations from the source instead of trusting generated timestamps.

The Case Study

Problem
Useful ideas are scattered across long videos. Summaries are easier to skim, but can lose context or point to a timestamp that does not support the claim.
What I Built
A pipeline that turns transcripts into source-linked topic pages, with a desktop and browser reader. Different transcription adapters feed the same pipeline; matching quoted wording to the transcript produces the citation locations.
How It Works
AI assists with organization and synthesis. Import checks require quoted wording to match the transcript and calculate timestamps from those matches. The reader keeps the source accessible beside the interpretation.
Where It Stands
Implemented for personal use with multiple creator libraries. This case study describes the workflow without publishing the private library, transcripts, or source code.
Evidence and Limits
  • An import dry run caught altered capitalization in quoted wording and required correction before import. Source checking constrains the AI output.
  • A matching quote establishes traceability, not the truth of the speaker’s claim or the completeness of a summary.
  • No claim of measured learning improvement or fully autonomous research is made.
