The Case Study

Problem
Capturing useful ideas is easier than turning them into a coherent body of knowledge. Notes accumulate across sources, while related ideas, important highlights, and material worth practicing stay disconnected.
What I Built
A source-import pipeline and reader with bilingual topic pages, themed sections, highlighted passages, dated digests, and language practice decks. Review flows let me internalize external material, approve assistant proposals by topic, and undo an import.
How It Works
Configured OneNote sources are exported locally and mirrored into structured text. An assistant workflow updates topic pages incrementally while preserving source relationships and review state. The same reader supports local access and an encrypted hosted copy. Publication checks screen content before it is synced.
Where It Stands
The reader and learning workflows are implemented for personal use, with a local editing path and an encrypted reading copy. This portfolio describes the system and its design decisions without exposing the notebook, personal topics, study material, or access details.
Evidence and Limits
  • AI-assisted synthesis is an explicit workflow; organizing a source does not establish that its claims are correct.
  • Local review and practice tools differ from the encrypted hosted reader. There is no unrestricted public demo.
  • No measured improvement in retention or learning is claimed. Personal notes and source excerpts are not published here.
