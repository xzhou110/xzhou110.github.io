XuSeak To-Do Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

XuSeak To-Do is a to-do, habit and idea app made by XuSeak. This policy says what the app keeps, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

The Short Version

  • Without an account, everything you create stays on your iPhone. The only thing the app sends is a check for app updates (below).
  • With the optional XuSeak Account, your email address, the name you choose and the things you create are stored on our server, so they can be backed up and synced to your other devices.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time, inside the app.

What Stays on Your iPhone

When you use the app without signing in, these stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us:

  • your lists, to-dos, habits, ideas, and your name and avatar
  • your preferences (theme, sounds and the like) and Daily Luck

Some things stay on your iPhone even when you are signed in:

  • unfinished idea drafts and Daily Luck
  • the Home Screen widget's copy of today's to-dos, including anything you check off on the widget before the app next opens, kept in the app's own storage on the phone
  • the diagnostics log (Settings › Diagnostics). It records what the app did (opened, synced, a problem occurred), never what you wrote, and it stays on your iPhone. It leaves the phone only if you tap Share Log and choose where to send it.

What the XuSeak Account Stores

If you choose to sign in, we store:

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code we email you, and to identify your account
The name and avatar you choose, if anyTo show them in the app on each of your devices (an avatar is one of the app's preset icons, never a photo)
Your lists, to-dos (with their dates and stars), habits and check-ins, and saved ideasTo back them up and keep your devices in sync
Your settings: theme, Show Task Counts, Sounds, Haptics and the likeSo the app looks and sounds the same on each of your devices
An account ID and the times your data changedTo keep your data apart from everyone else's and to sync correctly

We use this data only to provide these features.

Who Helps Us Run It

Two service providers process account data only to provide the service:

  • Supabase stores the account and synced data, on servers in the United States.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in code emails.

Neither may use your data for its own purposes.

App updates. When the app opens, it asks Expo's servers whether a newer version of its code is available, and downloads it if so. That request carries the app's version and, like any internet request, your network address. It never carries anything you wrote. This happens with or without an account. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, each account's data can be read and changed only by that signed-in account.

How Long We Keep It, and Deleting It

Your account data stays until you delete it.

To delete your account and its cloud data, go to Settings › Back Up and Sync › Delete XuSeak Account. This removes your account and its data from our server. It also removes the signed-in copy on that device. Lists you made without signing in stay on your iPhone.

You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to the address below.

Your Choices

You can:

  • see and change everything we store, inside the app
  • stop syncing at any time (Pause Sync)
  • delete your account

For any request about your data, write to us; we answer within 30 days.

Children

XuSeak To-Do is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the effective date above. If a change affects what we collect, we say so in the app's release notes before it applies.
