XuSeak To-Do Help

XuSeak To-Do keeps your day in one calm place: Today, your lists, habits and ideas, with a Home Screen widget, built to use both of iPhone Duo's displays.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one, and it all stays on your iPhone. The optional XuSeak Account backs up your lists, habits and ideas, and syncs them to your other devices.

How do I sign in?

  1. Go to Settings › Back Up and Sync.
  2. Enter your email and tap Continue with Email.
  3. Type in the code we email you. If it doesn't arrive in a minute, check your spam folder; the sender is XuSeak.

How do I add the widget?

  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen, then tap Edit › Add Widget.
  2. Find XuSeak To-Do › Today.
  3. Pick small, medium or large.

Open the app once first, so the widget has something to show. While your iPhone is locked, the widget hides to-do names.

Can I check a to-do off on the widget?

Yes, in the medium and large sizes: tap its box. Tap again to undo. The app catches up the next time it opens, and your other devices after that. Tap a to-do's name to open it in the app.

How does the app use iPhone Duo?

On the open iPhone Duo, Today, Ideas and Calendar show two panes side by side. Closed, the app uses a single column.

How do I remove a to-do or a whole list?

  • A to-do: swipe it left, and confirm.
  • A list: use its ⋯ menu › Remove List. That removes the list and everything in it, so the app always asks first.

Why does a starred to-do sit under a date?

A star keeps a to-do first among the ones due at the same time. Starring it doesn't move it ahead of things due sooner.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Back Up and Sync › Delete XuSeak Account. This removes your account and its cloud data. Lists you made without signing in stay on your iPhone.

Something went wrong. What should I send?

Go to Settings › About › Report a Problem, tell us what happened, and tap Continue in Mail. Your words and the app's log go into an email to us that you see before you send. The log records what the app did, never what you wrote.
