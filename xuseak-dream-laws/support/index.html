Dream Laws Help

Dream Laws: Card Roguelike is a one-thumb card game for iPhone. You slip into strangers' dreams, and every dream plays by its own Law.

Questions

What is free, and what does the Full Game unlock?

The Last Train dream is free, with two dreamwalkers, the Sleepwalker and the Insomniac, every Depth and the daily dream. The Full Game is a single one-time purchase that opens The Chase, The Endless Exam and the Lucid Dreamer, their Depths and the full daily rotation. It buys content, never power: no ads, no energy, no loot boxes. The price shows in the game in your own currency.

How do I buy the Full Game?

Tap a locked dream in the Night City, or the Lucid Dreamer, and the Unlock screen shows the price. Apple handles the payment with your Apple Account.

I bought it, but the dreams are still locked.

  1. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Connect to the internet once.
  3. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. The Unlock screen has the same button.

If it still shows locked, write to us with the date of purchase; never send your receipt's card details.

Where are my runs saved?

On your iPhone only. There are no accounts or cloud saves. They move to a new phone through your phone's own backup, and deleting the game deletes them.

Continue shows an older map than a new run.

A run keeps the map it started with, so an update's new routes appear in your next new run. Your saved run is not lost.

The text is too small, or too large.

The game follows your iPhone's text size. In the game's Settings › Text Size you can set it larger or smaller for the game alone.

The screen shakes or moves too much.

Settings › Reduce Motion follows your iPhone's setting, or set it to Always On. Screen Shake and Background Motion have their own switches.

Can I turn the sound off?

Settings has separate volumes for music and effects, and a switch for haptics.

How does the game use iPhone Duo?

On the open iPhone Duo the game fills the inner display with a wider board; closed, it plays one-handed on the outer display.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are refunded by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Something went wrong. What should I send?

Write to the address below with what happened, the dream and dreamwalker you were playing, and the version line from Settings › About (for example "Version 1.0.0 (Build 2)").
