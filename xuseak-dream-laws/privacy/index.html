Dream Laws Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Dream Laws: Card Roguelike is a card game for iPhone made by XuSeak. This policy says what the game keeps, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

The Short Version

  • Everything you do in the game stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell data.
  • The one purchase, the Full Game, goes through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The only thing the game sends is a check for app updates when it opens (below).

What Stays on Your iPhone

These are kept in the game's own storage on your iPhone and are never sent to us:

  • your runs: the dream, the dreamwalker, your deck, your route and where you are in it
  • your progress: the Journal, Depths reached, daily dream results, cards and items you have found
  • your settings: text size, sounds, motion, handedness and the like

They move to a new iPhone only through your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer). Deleting the game deletes them.

The Full Game Purchase

The Full Game is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we receive no name, card or billing details. The game keeps a note on your iPhone that you own the Full Game and confirms it with the App Store when the game opens, so that Restore Purchases works on a new phone and a refunded purchase is removed. Refunds are requested from Apple.

App Updates

When the game opens, it asks Expo's servers whether a newer version of its code is available, and downloads it if so. That request carries the game's version and, like any internet request, your network address. It also carries a random token that tells installations apart; it identifies neither you nor your phone. It never carries anything about your runs or settings. Without an internet connection the game plays as it is.

The Browser Version

A version of the game runs in a web browser at xzhou110.github.io. It keeps its saves in that browser's own storage on your device, and sends nothing to us. The pages are served by GitHub, whose own privacy statement applies to the serving of the site.

What We Do Not Do

  • no accounts, sign-in or cloud saves
  • no advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites
  • no sharing of data with anyone, except where the law requires it

Children

Dream Laws is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the effective date above. If a change affects what the game sends, we say so in the game's release notes before it applies.
